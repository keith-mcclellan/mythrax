---
title: Bug: Logic flaw in Arbor ConvergenceDetector causing premature false convergence signals
labels: [bug,agent-found]
---

**Severity:** Medium

**File:** `mythrax-core/src/cognitive/arbor.rs`
**Line:** 181

**Description:**
The `ConvergenceDetector::record_score` method evaluates the gradient over the history window. However, there is a known logic flaw: `ConvergenceDetector`'s early return logic based on `window_size` limits can prematurely trigger false convergence signals by evaluating gradients on an insufficient number of intervals (e.g., `delta_visits = 1.0`). A window size of 2 is allowed by `window_size.max(2)`. This means it can trigger `ParadigmShift` or `Stagnant` after just two visits, which is highly noisy.

**Minimal Reproducible Scenario:**
Initialize `ConvergenceDetector::new(2)`. Call `record_score(50.0)` then `record_score(50.0)`. It will return `Stagnant` or `ParadigmShift` after just 1 step, prematurely halting search.

**Suggested Fix:**
Enforce a minimum `delta_visits` (e.g., at least 3 or 4) before computing `score_velocity`, or increase the minimum `window_size` to something like 5.
